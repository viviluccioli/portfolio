Key Patterns:
  • Provider × Payor Performance:
    • BC2 × Humana leads on all metrics: 100% success, 95.5% OCR, lowest silent failure rate (21.2%). However, this is based on only 52 observations.
    • BC1 × UHC is the weakest performer: 67.6% OCR, 49.3% silent failure rate, and the only combo with agent timeouts and 403 portal errors. The Oct 28–30 dip maps almost entirely to BC1 × UHC failures.
    • Physical Health Clinic 1 × IEHP shows strong OCR (91.5%) but only 76.3% success—pointing to a structural credential issue rather than data quality.
  • Time Trends:
    • BC1 × UHC has been tested the longest, dating back to when OCR accuracy was very poor. Other clinic-payor combos were introduced later. It's unclear whether early poor performance reflects immature OCR technology/configuration at the time, or an inherent issue with UHC's portal.
    • OCR accuracy was poor across the board initially but improved significantly after 09/27.
    • Success rate improvements began earlier than OCR accuracy gains.
  • Caveats: Imbalanced observation counts across provider-payor combos limit direct comparisons.
