⚠ Silent Failures
A silent failure is a submission where agent_status = SUCCESS but complete_match_false_count > 0 — the agent completed the portal flow but submitted at least one incorrectly extracted field. Invisible to standard success metrics.
Critical Finding: 306 of 683 "successful" submissions (44.8%) contained ≥1 incorrectly OCR-extracted field. 118 were Severe (≥50% of fields wrong). The 90% headline success rate materially overstates true submission quality.
